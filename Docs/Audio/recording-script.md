# Lighthouse Keepers — Voice Recording Script

94 lines, 4 voice memos. Say the **number**, wait a second, say the **line**, then stay quiet for **2 seconds** before the next number. Mess up? Say **"again"** and repeat that line — the last try counts.

Lines 19, 23, 24, 25, 26 and 43 (in the game files but never played) and retired line 6 are left out on purpose. Some lines were reworded to sound natural (contractions such as "Water's at your knees"); the captions get updated to match when these are imported.

### MEMO 1 — Normal voice (calm, tired old keeper)

**1.** Keeper, you hear me? Storm's right on top of you. Keep that light burning till dawn. Lose the light, or let the water take the tower, and we're all done for.  
*(Serious. Like talking over wind.)*

**2.** Fix tonight's jobs. Sleep upstairs when they're done.  
*(Plain, helpful.)*

**3.** Three nights. Keep the light burning and the water down. Bring every ship home.  
*(Determined.)*

**4.** There's a pipe giving out downstairs. Grab the wrench off the bench.  
*(A little urgent.)*

**5.** Hold grip to take the wrench. Bring its head to the bolt, then pull toward you.  
*(Slow and clear, like teaching.)*

**6.** Hold grip. Bring it to the bolt and pull toward you. Push back, pull again.  
*(Like a coach. Pull... push back... pull.)*

**7.** Leave the wrench, keeper. Follow the lamps to your next job.  
*(Normal.)*

**8.** We saw your light last night. Another front's coming in. Generator's down again.  
*(Happy at first, then worried.)*

**9.** Generator's dead. Read the plate on the side. Prime it, choke it, pull.  
*(Quick and short.)*

**10.** Fuel's running low. Line up the lantern and send the storm warning.  
*(Worried.)*

**11.** Still with us, keeper? Check the board. It's going to be a long one.  
*(Tired, a tiny bit funny.)*

**12.** The pipe needs you. Take the wrench downstairs.  
*(Normal.)*

**13.** Next, the generator. Follow the lamps upstairs.  
*(Normal.)*

**14.** Head to the radio. Send the storm warning.  
*(Normal.)*

**15.** Up to the lantern, keeper. Light it and line up the marks.  
*(Normal.)*

**16.** Check the breakers beside the generator.  
*(Normal.)*

**17.** Jobs done. Grip your bunk upstairs.  
*(Relieved.)*

**18.** Follow the hiss, keeper. Wrench on the bolt. Pull.  
*(Patient.)*

**19.** The plate on the generator. Fuel, prime three, choke, pull three, choke down.  
*(Like reading a list.)*

**20.** Check the breakers. You can't run everything at once.  
*(Patient.)*

**21.** Get up to the lantern. Light it, then turn the wheel till the marks line up.  
*(Patient.)*

**22.** Four steps on the radio. Power, tune, code, then hold the mic.  
*(Like reading a list.)*

**23.** Work's done. Bunk's on the third floor.  
*(Tired.)*

**24.** Not yet. There's still work to do.  
*(A little strict.)*

**25.** That'll do it.  
*(Pleased. Short.)*

**26.** There she goes.  
*(Relieved.)*

**27.** Light's burning.  
*(Proud, quiet.)*

**28.** That'll hold. Get to your bunk, keeper. You'll need it.  
*(Warm and tired.)*

**29.** We see your light, keeper. Every ship made it through. Get some rest.  
*(Emotional. Slow. Happy.)*
