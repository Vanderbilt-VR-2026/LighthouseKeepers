# Headset test — timed starter, useful partner, belt radio and Night 1 rehearsal

Both human players need **LK-S5-TRAINING1 / LK-Lab-Coop-v47**. Install only; open the game yourself when ready. Start with normal Night 1 rather than DEV Night 3, because the first night now teaches every working station. DEV Night 2/3 is useful afterwards for targeted checks.

## Changes to look for

- Generator: tilt the fuel can, then make three physical cord pulls in the gauge’s marked band. No primer or choke in the campaign. Misses cough but keep successful progress. Let the cord recoil before pulling again.
- Night 1: pipe → refuel/start generator → light beacon → tune/code/transmit on the radio → steer the first ship → sleep. The first ship waits for your aim; it cannot wreck during this lesson. No enemies or fail timer in the tutorial.
- Owen: after Night 1, he automatically works the complementary duty. Human Lightkeeper gets a Machinist helper who handles engine/pipe/panel; human Machinist gets a Lightkeeper who reads/prepares the radio and tracks the beam. He waits for your walkie-trigger clearance before sending the ship. You can override with Follow/Wait or take a tool yourself. He is a scripted helper, not a general AI teammate.
- Walkies: release at your right hip to clip to the belt; grip to retrieve; release away from your hip to drop normally. Existing trigger communication and helper-code reply remain.
- Possession: mouth clearance through the beard and dark wet eye trails are improved; accepted body, arms, legs and camera remain.
- Earlier feedback fixes are included: eye-height briefing, less routine harbor chatter, stable walkie palm attachment, F2 lantern, no permanent Owen slate, corrected floating guards/fittings and radio supports. Narration stays text-only until the final recordings.

## Test the full route

1. **Lobby/arrival:** HOST / PLAY ALONE, choose a duty, START. Reopen Watch Menu → Briefing if already skipped. Check standing/seated height and recentering; it should be in front of you, not at the ceiling. Read/skip/reopen all three pages. No camera movement.
2. **Body and recall:** look down, raise both arms, crouch and turn your left wrist. Check watch/cuff clearance. Tap B for immediate current objective and last caption; hold B; try the objective-height setting and wrist recall near a wall.
3. **Pipe:** grip the wrench, bring it to the active bolt and pull/return the ratchet. No trigger lock and no solo clamp. Check visible clockwise turn, clicks and next-bolt glow. Drop the wrench, including near water; verify recovery.
4. **Generator:** follow the next objective to F2. Tilt the red can over the filler, set it down (it should fall normally, not float), then grip the cord and complete each pull while the needle is inside the marked band. Intentionally miss once: successful strokes must remain. Release/recoil between pulls; the third good pull starts the engine. Watch power/pump/lamps respond. Report whether the band feels readable and fair.
5. **First convoy:** light the beacon, then use the console power, tune, code buttons and microphone as instructed. Transmission should clear the ship. Actually turn the wheel: briefly miss, recover and hold the beam on her lights. The tutorial should wait instead of wrecking her. Confirm the HUD then sends you to the F3 bunk and sleep advances the night.
6. **Radio grip and belt:** use each hand, walk and turn while holding a walkie. Clip it at the right hip, leave both hands free, crouch/turn, retrieve it and drop it away from the belt. Check jitter, unwanted clipping and accidental belt snaps. Trigger should show the helper’s fetched code/status, not an invented answer.
7. **Night 2 lantern and scene:** grip the supported F2 lantern, use trigger to change the wick, check LOW/HIGH and oil, then hang it on its hook. Follow the F2 summons. In solo, Owen is possessed. Look for eye roll, wet dark trails, open mouth and bounded lift without ceiling clipping. Grip his offered hand; release/regrip once. Gates should reopen and he should resume useful movement. Your own camera must never float.
8. **Automatic partner — Lightkeeper player:** after the lesson, let Owen work the engine and repairs. He should walk to controls, carry/tilt the can and start the engine. Where the valve is required, hold it while he tightens. Try Wait, then resume. Take a tool to interrupt him; check that he yields instead of fighting your grip.
9. **Automatic partner — Machinist player:** use a fresh focused Night 2/3 run with the other duty. Do the machinery work while Owen gets the House code and prepares F4. He should wait for your explicit walkie-trigger clearance, then transmit and reach the beacon wheel. Watch him on stairs and report any stuck position and how long it lasts.
10. **Night 3 authentication:** repair in the displayed order. Read the House telegram or request the code Owen actually fetched. At F4, CHALLENGE the call, compare the reply, then ACCEPT matching / REJECT wrong. A wrong answer should let you recover. Confirm the next objective remains clear. The new bunk/hunt/hiding sequence is NOT in this build.
11. **World/audio:** inspect F3 stair edge, F4 radio/glass supports, beacon fittings and balcony entrance. Listen for the looping soundtrack, bounded intense accents, rain zone transitions and heartbeat; no AI voice or constant status chatter. Check whether any needed instruction disappears or becomes stale. In an optional DEV Night 4 run, the helper should shed load and reset the small-budget panel without repeatedly tripping it; later-night campaign acceptance remains pending.
12. **Two humans:** same network version on both devices. Join/start, see bodies/hands, exchange speech nearby and across floors, use walkies, valve/bolts, guest starter, radio/beam split, both directions of revival, shared sleep and leave/rejoin. A second human should replace Owen; leaving should restore solo help without losing task progress. Also try HOST / PLAY ALONE with Wi-Fi off.

## What to say out loud

One observation per sentence: **night → floor → nearest object → role/mode → hand/button/action → actual result → expected result**. Include exact HUD wording, whether it repeats and how long you waited. Example: “Night 2, F2 engine, solo Lightkeeper: Owen picked up the can, then stopped beside the filler for twenty seconds.” For clipping, say where you are looking from and take a screenshot if convenient. Tell me what feels good too, so it stays intact.
