# Quest playtest — House arrival, rescue, radio and soundtrack

This checklist covers the source containing the illustrated House briefing, Night 2 possession/revive, Night 3 call authentication and the new continuous music bed. The final delivery receipt beside the soundtrack evidence identifies the exact installed APK. Network: **LK-S5-ARRIVAL1 / LK-Lab-Coop-v42**; both players need this build. Installed without launching; open Lighthouse Keepers yourself.

## What changed

- Three-page briefing now opens **inside the House**, after START, rather than in the lobby. New generated wartime illustrations show the setting and duties; the last page shows real controller inputs. Back/Next/Skip/Finish; reread from Watch Menu. Initial danger clocks wait for both readers. Late joining does not pause a running watch.
- Night 2's caption-only Unknown summons both keepers to F2. Gates close, wall streaks appear, the bot (solo) or guest (co-op) is possessed, and gripping their offered hand teaches revival. The lesson pauses danger clocks. The visible body can rise; the player's camera never does.
- Night 3 adds CHALLENGE / ACCEPT / REJECT to the F4 radio. Match the reply against the House telegram or helper slate. Genuine replies have two taps; Unknown gives a wrong symbol. Wrong decisions can be retried. These decisions do not automatically switch machinery off.
- The supplied soundtrack now loops for about 6:37, with a four-second seam overlap. The earlier sample's exact 1:11–1:16 overlays danger beats. The bed keeps playing; accents do not stack or restart. Lobby is quieter; speech/chat ducking remains.
- Existing roles, bot support, lantern, tracked hands/body, ships and physical station controls are retained. No new AI narration. Spoken recordings come later.

Open the installed app → PLAY → HOST / PLAY ALONE → choose Lightkeeper or Machinist → START. For co-op the other player uses JOIN CREW and the displayed code; host starts once joined. First do Night 1 normally. To revisit a feature later, HOST / PLAY ALONE has **DEV: SELECT NIGHT 2** and **DEV: SELECT NIGHT 3**; choose one, then START. This build is a focused Nights 1–3 check, not a claim that the full five-night campaign is finished.

## Twelve checks, in order

1. **Lobby and arrival:** see hands/body, choose a duty and start. The briefing should appear in the House, not cover the lobby. Check that the solo helper is present; in co-op identify your partner and exchange a sentence each way.
2. **Briefing:** read context → duties → controls. Try Back and Next. Describe whether the artwork, text, buttons and placement make sense. Let one player finish while the other reads: danger should wait. Finish or Skip, then reopen it from Watch Menu. Report anything hidden behind walls or awkward to reach.
3. **Recall:** tap B for the current objective and last caption; hold B, then release. Try wrist glance/X/Y and Objective height in Watch Menu. Does it appear immediately, stay below your view and remain readable at the pipe and generator?
4. **Night 1 pipe:** GRIP the wrench, bring it to the active bolt and pull toward yourself. No trigger lock. Complete three bolts; test a normal drop/regrab. In co-op, follow the two valve/bolt roles; solo should not require the old clamp. Say whether the next control and next job are obvious.
5. **Night 1 completion:** watch the preview ship and use F3 sleep when the board allows it. In co-op both keepers must rest. Check the wake-up objective and transition to Night 2. Night 1 currently remains pipe/preview/sleep; the expanded generator/radio rehearsal is not implemented.
6. **Night 2 lantern and engine:** find and GRIP the lantern, use trigger to cycle OFF/LOW/HIGH, check useful light/oil/placement. Repair the engine using the current sequence: tilt fuel can → primer squeezes → choke closed → starter pulls → choke open. The simpler timed-pull replacement is still pending. Describe the exact object if a control floats, clips, is unclear or does not follow your hand.
7. **Night 2 summons:** follow the Unknown caption to the actual generator room on F2. Let both keepers enter. Check the sealed exits, threat caption, dark wall streaks and possessed body. In solo the bot is affected; in co-op the guest. Check eye/jaw/tear readability and ceiling clearance. Your camera must not be lifted or rotated.
8. **Revive:** follow the offered hand cue and hold GRIP; briefly release/regrip. Progress should resume, the partner should recover, and both exits should open. Report hand reach, whether you understood the action, or any trapped state. The lesson deliberately has no rescue deadline.
9. **Night 3 shared information:** restore prerequisites, power/tune the F4 radio and obtain the four-symbol code from the House telegram/helper slate. Does the Machinist/Lightkeeper guidance tell each keeper what to do? Can you read the clue and relay it without guessing?
10. **Authenticate and aim:** directly GRIP CHALLENGE, compare the caller's reply, then GRIP ACCEPT for the matching Navy call or REJECT for Unknown. Try a wrong decision once and challenge again. Prepare the radio early: the current false call is tied to the first ship's approach and is cancelled after it passes. Keep the beacon aimed for the ship; decisions should never silently switch it off.
11. **Music, storm and world:** new music should remain beneath captions and danger. Listen for the five-second accent at a blackout, Night 2 threat, ship danger window or visible scare; clustered events should not spam it. Visit F4 inside and outside. Describe music/rain balance, abrupt jumps, distortion, a distracting loop seam, or details that feel good. No AI voice should read captions.
12. **End and co-op recovery:** finish remaining Night 3 tasks and check sleep/outcome. If testing with two people, also try one leave/rejoin and ensure duties, call state and shared work survive. The new bunk scare → hunter → hiding/hold-breath sequence is NOT present yet; do not spend time searching for it.

These are risk areas, not confirmed failures: VR folder scale/button reach; read/rejoin gating; radio control reach and clue readability under pressure; possessed hand reach and body clearance; captions competing with objectives; balance of a new soundtrack against rain and spatial sound. Worn performance and human voice remain unverified. The radio's first-ship-only trigger can miss the false-call beat if you reach the station late. Full-run timing is not proven by focused fixtures.

Known unfinished: the shared scare kit's gaze start/replay skip/local dark-red muffled possession view; Night 3 bunk/hunter/hiding; expanded Night 1 all-station tutorial; new timed-starter mechanic. Do not treat those as present. Existing apparitions are separate from the disabled full hunter.

## Wispr Flow feedback

Talk naturally when you notice something; no need to narrate every footstep. For a problem, include **night, floor, object/side, solo/co-op and role, action/button/hand, expected result, actual result**. For visual clipping, say what intersects what and where you were standing. For audio, say what else was happening and whether it was too loud, too quiet, repetitive, late or missing.

Examples: “Night 2, F2, solo Machinist: I held left grip on his offered hand; progress stopped when he rose.” / “Night 3, outside F4: the music accent hit with the ship and felt good, but covered the rain.” Also say what you like and why; that protects the parts that work. Mark a blocker as “stuck”; a cosmetic issue as “visual only.” If stuck, stop and describe it before restarting. Short clips/screenshots are helpful for intermittent visuals but not required.
