# Day 2 generator-room bulb pop (#144)

During the child’s witnessed turn, the existing Floor 2 bulb briefly brightens once, emits at most 16 small sparks and plays the existing breaker pop; supported controllers receive a short pulse. The effect uses the existing URP spark material, adds no realtime light and does not move the camera. The host's already-synchronized scene age drives presentation; a client arriving after the pop does not replay the particles or sound. The interval and count are in `HorrorTuning`. This adds no synchronized field and the bedtime scene remains disabled pending completion and headset acceptance.

A controlled, muted Play Mode fixture used the Quest URP asset in the Editor, staged the Turn beat and checked the bulb, particle count, late presentation and light count. **8/8 checks passed** (`results/checks.txt`). I looked at `images/before.png` and `images/burst.png`: the pop reads as a small orange spark at the actual room bulb. The fixture does not prove headset visibility, audio level, haptics, GPU cost or the complete scene. 63 focused EditMode checks also passed (NightmareIntegration 5, ScareSequence 22, QualityBaseline 3, SurvivalMode 10, VoicePass 23). No Android rendering claim is made.

To reproduce, open Unity 6000.3.23f1, set `EditorUtility.audioMasterMute=true`, run `python3 artifacts/s5/scare-scenes/start.py`, then invoke `BulbBurstProbe.Begin` through the Pipeline bridge with a fresh absolute output directory. Keep `#144` open until the remaining scene checks in [Next.md](../../../../Design/Next.md) are complete.
