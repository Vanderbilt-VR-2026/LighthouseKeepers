# Integrated build: ten headset checks

Both players must use **LK-S5-INTEGRATED1**. APK: `Builds/LighthouseKeepers-Integrated.apk`. Use headphones on the Mac for voice checks.

1. In the lobby, read the network version. Press PLAY SOLO. Confirm full VR, normal standing height, familiar hands and the House board.
2. Turn your head away from the objective: it should stay in the world. Press X/Y or glance at the wrist to place it in front again. Listen for the recorded arrival and new status guidance.
3. Pick up the wrench with GRIP. Walk across the bench area and doorway: no head pop or flicker. At the pipe, set the solo valve clamp first.
4. Bring the held wrench to the active top bolt. It should snap without trigger. Pull toward yourself, reset and repeat; see clockwise turning/sinking. Try each hand, then drop/recover the wrench.
5. Walk F1→F2→F3→F4. Look slowly around each doorway/stair landing. Check the former Support foot and F3/F4 edge barriers without leaning outside your safe play space.
6. At F4, look through the lantern glass and along the gallery. Eli’s sea/mountains/sky should stay visible as you turn. Inside uses roof rain; outside should become louder, clearer exterior rain. Compare F1 outside too.
7. Try Night2 generator/breakers and Night3 beacon→radio. Check grace at arrival, clear next steps, power/flood effects, pole and sleep. Pause at each requested profiling viewpoint while measurements run.
8. Return to lobby. Host creates a crew; guest enters that code in CREW OPTIONS, closes keypad, JOIN CREW, SIGNAL READY. Host readies and begins. Both should see the other keeper.
9. One keeper holds the pressure valve while the other repairs. Release briefly: tightening pauses, progress stays. Swap roles. Speak nearby/across floors; test a bench radio by trigger and by holding it near the head. Test mute.
10. Complete the shared job and both grip the bed. Confirm the same next night, then guest returns to lobby and rejoins the active code. Note any mismatch, lost audio, clipping or hitch.

For profiling, stand still about25 seconds at House board, F1 pipe, F2 generator, F4 radio and gallery facing the sea; repeat the gallery on Night3. Continuous motion/comfort and audio intelligibility require the person wearing the headset, not screenshots alone.
