Use matching **LK-S4-MASTER-B6** builds for both players. These are acceptance steps, not completed results.

1. Start offline PLAY SOLO. Confirm full VR, correct height, tan aligned hands, no vignette/grain. Read the three short loop beats; find the wrench without asking another person.
2. Hold GRIP on the wrench. Bring it to the lit bolt without touching trigger. Make short arm pulls, reposition, and repeat. Try both hands; confirm clockwise turn/sink, click and rumble.
3. Drop the wrench in water; confirm recovery. Walk from House to pipe without head bumps or doorway flicker. Night 1 must not kill a learner.
4. Finish the pipe. Confirm “leave wrench”/sleep guidance and path lighting; find the bunk without returning to the House board. Read the slate and grip the bunk.
5. Wake into Night 2. The first job appears immediately at the bunk. On outage, follow one generator instruction at a time: fuel if needed, primer ×3, choke closed, starter ×3, choke open. Extra primer actions must not punish you.
6. Watch the wrist timepiece, waterline marks and warning strip. Ask for status with X/Y and the PA button. Hear the PA while climbing stairs; distinguish approaching beacon danger from water danger.
7. Use both upper pole entrances. Grip to descend, release to pause, regrip to continue. Confirm comfortable easing and landing, no rotation; use stairs for F2→House. Walk the gallery perimeter without falling.
8. Complete Night 3 at a first-timer pace. Record actual task/travel/idle times; tune only from evidence. Try a deliberately neglected later night, verify a clear cause and retry.
9. Start a crew with a matching second player. Confirm avatars and clear two-way voice, nearest-job/partner guidance, guest task reflected on both, and visible pole descent.
10. Both grip the bunk; both wake together. Guest returns to lobby and rejoins the running watch. Record GPU/CPU, draws, triangles and active audio voices on every floor, outside and during peak storm.

Targets: sustained 72 fps, GPU ≤11 ms, main CPU ≤9 ms, roughly ≤150 draws/eye, ≤300k visible triangles, ≤24 audio voices. No target is verified by the Mac screenshots or rules bot.
