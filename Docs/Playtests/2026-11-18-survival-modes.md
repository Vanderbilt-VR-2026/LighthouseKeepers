2. Arrival: the three-page briefing appears at your height and follows your head. Read or Skip. Collect your walkie, lantern, then the wrench if your job requires it. B recalls your current job; the wristwatch counts elapsed time.

4. Pipe F1: one keeper holds the pressure valve; the other tightens the three bolts. In solo, Owen takes the other job. Power the pump and confirm the water drains.
5. Radio: the House keeper reads the telegram code to the F4 operator. With Owen, use the walkie trigger to request his report. Tune, enter the code, hold the mic and HOLD TRIGGER to transmit. Speaking aloud is not required. The ship waits offshore until clearance.
6. Beacon: go OUTSIDE on F4. Light the beacon and turn the wheel by moving your hand around its rim. Deliberately miss for a while: the ship should wait, never wreck/end the run. Then keep the beam on it until it passes.
7. Recovery: finish the jobs and both rest on F3. Day 2 begins with all stations again, faster flood and tighter aiming. Check the partner remains synced after returning to the lobby and rejoining by code.
8. Loss: let water rise without repairing/pumping. Sustained high water should end the watch; Retry resets the same day. Ordinary time passing or a dark beacon must not end Survival. Falling into the sea should return you after ten seconds.
9. Audio / horror: Survival uses the original adaptive score, rain and machinery. No Drowned, Unknown, possession, horror music hit, or harbor voice. B/HUD instructions and Owen's text reports still work.
10. Campaign: return to the lobby and choose Campaign. The current horror game, narrative briefing and player-supplied score should return. This mode retains its existing failure rules.
