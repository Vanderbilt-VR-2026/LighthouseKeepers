# Quest playtest — briefing, tools and light

The briefing worked well. The wrench clipped, but the flashlight did not; the tool holders appeared to float in front of the body, and the radio holder clipped. The player did not understand why they were taking the wrench or how the work was split. They asked for the generator-room light to come on brighter with power, the flashlight to be 35% brighter, and repeated reminders that B recalls the objective.

Latest direction: replace the handheld flashlights and beanies with hard hats carrying automatically available forward-facing headlamps. Keep the wrench and radio holders connected to the body and usable in co-op. Prior reports of eye discomfort require a worn check of the brighter light; do not claim Quest comfort or performance from the Editor.
