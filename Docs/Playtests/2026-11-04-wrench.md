# Quest feedback — wrench ratcheting

The lead liked snapping the wrench onto the pipe and seeing the bolt animate, but found the continuous tightening motion physically difficult: rotating the controller through roughly 180 degrees was uncomfortable. Requested several short cranks that save tightening between strokes.

Cause: immersion repair retained progress when pulling away, but the reverse motion while still seated subtracted from the unfinished quarter-turn. A player resetting their wrist could undo the work they had just made.

Change: in the immersion repair mode, the first deliberate turn sets the tightening direction. Movement back toward a comfortable wrist pose freewheels; it never subtracts earned degrees or increases the leak. Several short strokes accumulate into the existing quarter-turn clicks and bolt completion. Pulling away also preserves partial progress. The wrench still follows the wrist around the socket; the bolt remains tightened while the handle resets. Either hand can work, with no base-hand rotation changes. Original feature-off repair remains unchanged.

Acceptance: complete bolts using only 30-degree out-and-back wrist strokes, with both hands; no progress loss on return or re-seating; stationary holding does not repair; guest repair updates the authoritative host. Headset comfort remains a user acceptance check. The pipe's first-bolt and idle-hint recordings now explain short turns and wrist resets.
