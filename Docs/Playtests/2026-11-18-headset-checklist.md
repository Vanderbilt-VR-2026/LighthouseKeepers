## How to narrate

Say **night → floor → exact object → hand/button → what you did → expected result → actual result**. Example: “Night 1, Floor 4 radio, right GRIP on the red lever. I lifted twice. It stayed off, then worked after releasing.” Mention whether it happens every time, and whether you are solo or with another person. Give likes too: describe the sound, object or moment so it can be preserved. Record a screenshot for clipping and say which side you approached from.
