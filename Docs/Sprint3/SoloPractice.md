# Solo practice

Choose **PLAY SOLO** on the main lobby board or in **CREW OPTIONS**. No host,
join code, ready signal, internet connection or second keeper is needed. If you
already pressed Host, Play Solo closes that connection before loading the game.

The lighthouse opens in offline practice. All stairs are accessible. Each floor's
instruction board has **DAY 1**, **DAY 2**, **DAY 3** and **RELAY** selectors.
Selecting a test resets its water, progress and failure state in place; it does
not move your tracked camera. Use **RETURN TO LOBBY** to leave.

1. In the lobby, try both Play Solo buttons across separate launches. Also try
   Host → Play Solo. Confirm there is no readiness or partner wait.
2. **DAY 1:** take the wrench, touch the pipe coupling and twist. You can repair
   without someone holding the brace. Check grip/release/regrip, progress and spray.
3. **DAY 2:** power starts off. Stroke the manual pump and compare with holding it
   still. Climb to the generator and grip/twist its control; it can recover without
   someone pumping below at the same time.
4. **DAY 3:** test the combined generator/pipe work yourself. Completed steps are
   retained through normal release/regrip. Choose a selector again for a fresh test.
5. **RELAY:** go to the radio floor and hold the radio to note its code. Climb to
   the lantern; the solo board keeps that code visible. Turn the matching switches
   and finish alone. A wrong switch resets only unfinished beacon progress.
6. If you run out of air, choose another test on the board or restart at the house
   result board. There is no artificial partner to rescue you. All stairs stay open.
7. Return to the lobby and verify the two-person crew flow still requires two
   ready keepers. Solo rules are scoped to the run entered through Play Solo.
