DAY 4 "THE FLOOD" + DAY 5 "THE LONG NIGHT" — story, mechanics, horror set pieces and the two final
chases. Read this all first, then build in order. Make the exact implementation decisions yourself, but
meet every rule and check here. It builds on S5-Day2-Day3-Scares.md: reuse its scare kit (door
lock/slam, lights out/burst, blood rain/bleeding walls, fake voice, possession float + bleeding eyes,
lunge, chase AI, blink/teleport, HUD alerts), A = run with stamina, X = hold breath, the fixed flare gun,
and revive by grabbing your partner's hand.
Rule zero: don't break anything that works. VR rules: never move or rotate the player's camera; no scares
inside the face (stop at least 0.5 m away); no strobing (flashes below 3 Hz); start each scene when the
player is looking toward it; scenes skippable on replay. Host owns all state; both players see the same
moment. Quest budget still applies (72 fps): big monsters are mostly silhouette, darkness and parts
(arms, a head, eyes), not huge detailed meshes. Bump the network version and report it.

SETTING REMINDER: WWII. The keepers guide American supply convoys past the rocks. The real harbor master
(Navy port office) always has radio static and answers the night's challenge word; the fake voice is
clean, too close and lies. The evil wants the light out so ships die. The dead it takes become its.

====================================================================================================
DAY 4 — "THE FLOOD"
====================================================================================================
STORY: last night the fake voice promised "Tomorrow you meet the water." Tonight the sea comes into the
tower. The sailors from the ship that exploded on Day 3 aren't gone; they're in the water now, and
they're its. Mid-night, the real harbor master finally confesses: he heard the last keepers screaming on
the radio and didn't send a boat because the convoy orders said no boats. He's ashamed. He won't make
that mistake again.

MECHANICS
- FAST FLOOD: the water rises much faster than before and starts already high. Floor 1 becomes black,
  waist-deep water. Lantern light reflects on the surface; you can't see what's under it. Triage the pump
  vs the beacon vs the generator under real pressure.
- HANDS IN THE WATER: in flooded areas, pale hands can grab a keeper's legs. Always telegraph first:
  bubbles, pale shapes sliding under the surface, a cold splash sound. Grabbed = you slow to a crawl and
  get pulled toward deeper water. Break free by shining the lantern on HIGH into the water, by your
  partner grabbing your hand and pulling, or by getting out of the water. If you're pulled all the way
  under → down/possessed → revive rules.
- Two convoy ships close together; the evil attacks the light during their windows.
- One flare tonight. Lantern oil is tight.

SCENE 1 — "THE PIPE" (the first big scare, early in the night)
- The pipe bursts in the flooded Floor 1. A keeper wades in, waist-deep, lantern hung on the hook,
  wrenching the bolts. Bubbles start rising around them.
- A drowned sailor's face floats up to the surface right beside the pipe, face-up, eyes closed, in a
  Navy uniform from last night's ship. Then its eyes open.
- Dozens of pale hands rise out of the black water all around. They grab. The keeper at the pipe is
  dragged backward through the water toward the dark end of the room (the movement is the world around
  them, done carefully for comfort, or a fade; never yank their camera). Their lantern falls and sinks,
  glowing, under the water.
- The partner has a few seconds to wade in and grab their hand and pull them out while hands grab at
  them too. Saved: both stagger out, the hands sink back. Failed: the keeper goes under → possessed →
  the partner must revive them on dry floor after the hands retreat (time limit). Solo: you must break
  free yourself (lantern HIGH at the water, or reach the stairs) before you're pulled under.
- After: the pipe still needs fixing. Every visit to Floor 1 now has that fear.

SCENE 2 — "THE GLASS" (during the first ship window)
- At the beacon, black wet handprints start appearing on the OUTSIDE of the lantern-room glass, one after
  another, climbing. Then many. Then a drowned face presses flat against the glass right in front of the
  lens, mouth open, eyes white. Wipe the glass/scare it off with the lantern beam while holding the ship.

DAY 4 CHASE — "THE DROWNED CREW" (climax of the night, after the second ship window)
- The water surges up the tower. Out of it come the dead crew from the Day 3 ship: several drowned
  sailors, soaked uniforms, life jackets, bleeding eyes, moving in jerks, climbing up the stairs after
  the keepers. This chase goes UP (the opposite of the Day 2 nightmare).
- HUD: "RUN — hold A", then "CLOSE THE HATCH". The keepers must climb floor by floor while the water
  rises behind them, slamming doors as they go. At the top of the stairs to Floor 3 there's a heavy iron
  hatch: one keeper holds it down while the other slides the bar across (both hands, physical). Solo:
  slower, it's possible but tight. Hands punch through the gap and grab at you while you close it.
- Caught = down. Both caught, or the hatch isn't closed in time → night failed, retry from the last
  checkpoint (decide a fair one).
- After the hatch holds: pounding from below, then scratching, then silence. The water drains.
- MORNING SCENE: on every step of the stairs, a set of dog tags, wet. The real harbor master, quiet: "They
  were boys. Every one of them."
- END TAUNT (fake voice, clean, at bedtime): "Tomorrow I come up myself."

====================================================================================================
DAY 5 — "THE LONG NIGHT" (the finale)
====================================================================================================
STORY: the biggest convoy of the war season, led by a troopship carrying thousands of soldiers, in the
worst storm yet. The evil stops hiding. It possesses the whole lighthouse and finally shows its true
form: a giant drowned thing made of everyone the sea has taken, sailors, the previous keepers, the child,
all fused together. If the light goes out, the troopship dies and the evil becomes stronger than ever.
The real harbor master finally does the right thing: he's coming out himself at dawn, orders or not.

MECHANICS
- Everything at once: fake radio calls (challenge them), sabotage, flooding with hands in the water,
  apparitions, the relentless chase pressure, triage between pump, generator and beacon.
- Two flares tonight: one for the middle of the night, one saved for the end (the game must make sure
  players know to save one).
- A checkpoint right before the final chase (the night is too long to replay from the start).

OPENING SCENE — "THE TOWER IS ALIVE"
- Players arrive for the watch and the tower is wrong. The walls on every floor bleed slowly. Every bulb
  pulses like a heartbeat, in sync with a heartbeat buzz in the controllers. The fake voice speaks
  through every speaker in the tower at once, overlapping itself: "This whole tower is my throat. And
  you're inside it." Then it all snaps back to almost normal, with a faint hum. The real harbor master
  through heavy static: tonight's convoy and the troopship, and "Whatever happens tonight, don't let
  that light go out."

MID-NIGHT SCENE — "THE OTHERS" (in a blackout)
- In the dark lantern room or stairwell, each lightning flash shows figures standing silently: the two
  previous keepers, the child, drowned sailors. Each flash they're closer. They only move when it's
  dark. At the last flash they're right around the keepers, a ring of bleeding faces, and then the
  lights come back and they're gone. (Cheap and terrifying: poses that change between flashes.)

FINAL CHASE — "THE TRUE FORM" (the climax)
1. THE RISE: during the troopship's approach, the generator dies one last time and the keepers have to
   restart it on Floor 2. As it roars back to life, the sea outside rises in a huge swell and the true
   form surfaces: a giant mass of drowned bodies and arms with a face that's many faces, red eyes. It
   slams into the tower; the whole tower shakes; windows burst.
2. RUN TO THE LIGHT: HUD: "RUN — get to the beacon". The keepers must climb to the top of the tower while
   it attacks the tower from outside: arms burst through windows and grab at them on the stairs, hands
   reach out of the bleeding walls, the lights die floor by floor, the stairs flood behind them, and the
   possessed previous keepers block routes and must be dodged. Relentless, but always a way through.
3. HOLD THE LIGHT: at the top, the beam must be held on the troopship while the true form climbs the
   outside of the tower toward the gallery. Its hand smashes over the railing; its face rises over the
   edge, red eyes, filling the view of the storm (at a safe distance, still far bigger than anything
   else in the game). It reaches for the lens. Co-op: one keeper holds the beam, the other must fight it
   off (the last flare, aimed at its eyes/mouth). Solo: hold the beam until the ship's last stretch, then
   turn and fire.
4. THE LAST FLARE: HIT → it screams with every voice it has stolen, burns, loses its grip and falls back
   into the sea. The beam stays on the ship; the troopship clears the rocks; ships' horns blow across
   the whole convoy. MISS, or the light goes out during the troopship window, or both keepers are
   grabbed → failure (the troopship wrecks / the keepers are taken), retry from the checkpoint before
   the final chase.

ENDING
- Dawn breaks. Gold light floods the tower. The bleeding walls dry and fade. On the rocks below, the true
  form's remains sink into the water as the sun hits them. Calm sea, gulls.
- A Navy boat comes to the dock: the real harbor master himself. The keepers walk down to meet it
  (player-controlled; no forced movement).
- FINAL STING: on the boat, the radio crackles... then clears. A perfectly clean, calm voice: "[lighthouse
  call sign], come in. Same time next storm." (Use the call sign the game already uses.) The
  lighthouse light behind them turns on by itself. Cut to black. Short credits (team + asset credits).
- FAILURE ENDING: the troopship's lights go under. The fake voice: "Thank you." Retry from the checkpoint.
