# Three faces of the Drowned

The same sea-born presence takes three original forms. The existing warning, safe placement, darkness, restoration and retreat rules apply to all of them. These are **non-contact apparitions**, not a completed hunter; pursuit, hiding and capture remain unfinished.

| Form | Silhouette | First scheduled variation | Body triangles |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Sea hag | Hunched back, wet hanging hair, one exposed hollow eye, ragged shift, long fingers | Night 2 generator fault | 6,042 |
| Soaked doll | Small nightdress, cracked pale face, uneven bob, bare feet, fixed stare | Night 3 generator fault | 5,588 |
| Gaunt creature | Tall narrow torso, ribs, hanging arms, long open jaw | Night 4 generator fault | 5,644 |

Later faults vary the form using the replicated night and fault index. Host, guest, retry and late join calculate the same form without another network field. One body renderer/material is active; all three meshes are built once and cached. The shared 68-triangle mist scales to the form’s height. No character lights, colliders, texture downloads or extra reflection passes. The existing local Sightings Off setting hides all forms/effects. Placement checks the correct face height and enough ceiling clearance; near-face exclusion remains.

The first screenshot review found inverted surface normals and a flat appearance, plus hair covering the hag’s face completely. Corrected winding, rounded shoulders, directional shading, restrained skin mottling and a gap in the hair were checked in a second graphics run. They are stylized original low-poly characters; human fear/comfort and Quest stereo readability are still device checks.
