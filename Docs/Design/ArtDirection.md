# Sprint 4 art direction
A salt-stained 1900s working lighthouse during a dangerous storm.
Whitewashed stone reveals dark masonry through chipped plaster.
Worn timber planks and stairs carry damp, dark seams.
Rust breaks through painted iron; brass catches small warm highlights.
Practical caged lamps make pools of amber light, never a uniform wash.
Cold blue window light separates the storm from the refuge indoors.
Deep corners hide detail while stations and stair routes remain readable.
Thin haze, drips and occasional dust suggest a building under strain.
The lantern uses clear reflective glass, concentric Fresnel rings and brass ribs.
Water is murky, moving and reflective without screen-space tricks.
Ocean crests, foam and a broken rocky inlet frame the island.
Night one starts with drizzle; later nights become progressively harsher.
Every prop suggests a keeper's previous watch and stays out of the path.
Protect Will's skybox and avatar body; blend through lighting and fog only.
