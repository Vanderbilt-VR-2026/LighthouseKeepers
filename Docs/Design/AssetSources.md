# Visual pass asset sources

All four PBR sets are Poly Haven assets under CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license . Downloaded from the official files API, 5 November 2026.

| Surface | Source | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Salt-dark plaster/stone | https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_stone_wall | 2K |
| Worn timber | https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_planks | 1K |
| Rusty painted iron | https://polyhaven.com/a/green_metal_rust | 1K |
| Rock / wet ground | https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry | 1K |

Diffuse and OpenGL normal maps are original downloads. Packed mask is derived from ARM: metallic→R, AO→G, zero→B, inverse roughness→A. Colour/masks use ASTC6x6, normals ASTC5x5 on Android; trilinear mipmaps and anisotropy4. No displacement or parallax. Brass is a tinted metal variant, not a claimed brass photoscan. Existing material GUIDs retained. All later generated meshes, water normals, haze and sound effects are original project code, not third-party downloads.

Plaster diffuse is desaturated and blended 60% toward a pale limewash; source normal is retained at 0.30 strength. Brass diffuse is a brightened grayscale metal derivative with a 0.86 metallic mask. Untouched downloaded originals remain in local artifacts.

## Original C4/C5 assets

The wave/ripple, glass, rain-streak, jet and wall-runoff shaders are original project code. `WaterRippleNormal.png` is a generated sinusoidal normal map, not downloaded artwork. Coastline rocks deform and combine Unity primitive geometry. Keeper props and rope coil are original low-poly geometry. No new avatar or skybox model/texture was imported. Chalk and doorway trickle clips are original procedural synthesis; existing creak, breaker and water clips are reused as documented in `WorldAudioGaps.md`.

## Sprint 5 coastal steamer (Phase 2.1)

Original project geometry generated by `SteamerGeometry` / `SteamerAuthoring`: curved 52 m hull, wheelhouse, funnel, masts, lifeboats, navigation lamps and painted names. No downloaded model, texture, font, image or audio in this asset. Vertex colours and two original URP shaders; baked meshes, 204-triangle distant light cluster, GPU vertex smoke/spray. Existing ocean/coast/sky assets are reused unchanged. No new third-party attribution required.

Sprint5 ship foghorn, thanks horn and wreck are original deterministic synthesis (`Tools/Audio/build_ship_sounds.py`), no sampled/third-party audio. Mono 22.05 kHz, Vorbis import, sample peak 0.68; existing SFX_Critical bus, spatial linear rolloff 25–320 m, source gain 0.35. Device loudness/spatial balance remains pending. Existing recorded P21–P24 are reused unchanged.
