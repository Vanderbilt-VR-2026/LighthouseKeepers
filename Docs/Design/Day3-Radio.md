# Day 3 — Don't trust the radio

WWII, Saltmere Navy port office. American supply convoys need Kettle Rock's beam. The last pair followed an unauthenticated call. Dialogue stays on screen until final recordings.

## Playable order

1. The Navy teaches the nightly four-symbol authentication code, using the existing House telegram and partner delivery. Its real calls begin with two taps and “Saltmere port office”. The sound is also captioned. A tell helps; the code is the proof.
2. Three grip buttons fitted to the F4 radio provide CHALLENGE / ACCEPT / REJECT. Challenge displays the caller's reply. Compare it with the telegram, then accept a matching reply or reject a mismatch. A wrong decision gives corrective feedback and allows another attempt. No call operates equipment automatically.
3. An impostor asks for the beacon to be extinguished during a ship approach. Its reply contains a wrong symbol. Its speaker tag remains **Unknown**. Expired calls are dropped, not announced after a ship has gone.
4. After repairs, the crew is asked to check the empty F3 bunk. The previous keeper sits up under a sheet only after the crew is looking at it. A short scene teaches the first warned hunt, hiding and holding breath. No camera movement, face contact or strobe; replay skip removes presentation, never the recovery objective.
5. The crew returns to guide the final supply ship. The ending acknowledges both the ship and what happened to the previous keepers.

## Shared scare kit and safety

Reuse Day 2's locks, lights, surface-bound stains, caption channel and possession presentation. Host owns scene phase, elapsed time and choices; guests only request actions. Body lift is visible to the partner; the possessed player's tracked head stays still, with a dark-red tint and muffled world sound. Pause dangerous clocks during scripted presentation, not indefinitely during normal play. Roster changes must release gates or regather. Scare scenes last 15–30 seconds; interactive rescue/hiding may take longer. Off/Low settings keep task progression possible.
