## Behavior and tuning

- EmergencyLighting remains the single interior-light controller. A smooth 2 s cycle scales normal intensity between 45% and 65%, with a mild orange/red tint. Alarm volume falls from 0.09 to 0.02 over 10 s; power restoration stops it and restores warm light over 1 s. No strobe or camera effect.
- WatchState owns the continuous beacon-dark deadline. Day02 is 90 s; Day03/04 are 75 s; Day01 disables it. Edit `beaconDarkLimitSeconds` on the day asset. House board counts down; failure latches, blocks repairs/generator/progression and offers same-day trigger retry. The existing HUD also shows loss/retry. Lantern, rotating spotlight and lens emission turn off with power, with original generated power-down/spin-up tones.
- `Assets/WatchDemo/Resources/PipeRepair.asset` contains all pipe tuning: A two quarter-turns, B three, C steady 3 s then one, 90° strokes, 65° ratchet return, 0.6 s seating, 18 cm jaw contact, 65% unfinished-progress retention. A 720°/s safety cap admits ordinary quick wrist turns; it is not an artificial work-speed limit. Each day/retry randomizes coupling order. Only the active coupling glows/sprays; completed turns/bolts survive release; inflow/spray reduce by thirds. Standing reach heights are 0.82/1.05/1.35 m. First-timer 40–60 s is a headset tuning target, not measured acceptance.
- Radio removes the six-switch auxiliary row, retaining 16 controls and replacing the gap with decorative service meters. Mounted numbered step strip: current amber, completed subdued green, future dim. Guide lamps identify the current control; the readout gives short instructions. Solo call code is large on the separate CodeSource. Out-of-step inputs do not undo accepted progress; wrong active code resets the sequence only. Held microphone/trigger transmission is unchanged.
- Hands, original shell, accepted generator model/mechanics/haptics/audio, flood authority, bed and day task lists are preserved. The pipe interaction is the requested exception to the former passive repair.
