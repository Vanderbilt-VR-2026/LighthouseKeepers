# Keeper footsteps

BootStep_01–04.wav are original synthesized boot impacts created for Lighthouse Keepers, with no third-party samples. Each is 0.24 seconds, mono, 24 kHz, 16-bit PCM. They combine a damped low thud, filtered sole noise, a short heel transient and a quieter scuff. Four distinct fixed noise seeds and resonances supply variation.

PlayerFootsteps adds subtle pitch/gain variation and routes playback through the Player mixer group. These are inexpensive stylized effects; final loudness and character should be auditioned on Quest alongside the storm mix.
